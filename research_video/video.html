Recommendations

🎓 Accounting students

  • Explore digital tools beyond the classroom
  • Join internships, workshops, and online courses
  • Regularly assess and improve your skills

🧑‍🏫 Educators

  • Spreadsheet exercises, simulations, and case-based activities
  • Training on available technologies
  • Practical assessments, not only written exams

🏛️ PLV administrators

  • Accounting software and functional computer labs
  • Faculty development
  • Curriculum reviews with alumni and industry
