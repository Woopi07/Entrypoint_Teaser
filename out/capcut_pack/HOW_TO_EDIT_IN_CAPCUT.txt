BEYOND TECHNICAL SKILLS - CapCut editing pack
=============================================

WHAT'S INSIDE
  0_finished_video_reference.mp4  The finished video (voice + music + subtitles), for reference only
  1_video_no_subtitles.mp4        The animation with NO subtitles and NO audio: put this on the main track
  2_voiceover.mp3                 Narration only
  3_music.mp3                     Background music only (no ducking applied)
  4_subtitles.srt                 Subtitles, imported as editable CapCut captions
  5_scenes/                       The same video cut into its 14 scenes, if you'd rather rearrange or replace scenes

CAPCUT DESKTOP / WEB (recommended)
  1. New project > Import: add 1_video_no_subtitles.mp4, 2_voiceover.mp3 and 3_music.mp3.
  2. Drag the video onto the timeline at 0:00. Drag 2_voiceover.mp3 below it, also at 0:00.
  3. Drag 3_music.mp3 to another audio track at 0:00 and lower its volume (about 20-30%)
     so the narration stays clear.
  4. Captions: Text > Captions/Subtitles > Import local captions (or "Import file")
     and choose 4_subtitles.srt. The captions are now editable text: change the font, size,
     color or wording in one place and it applies to all of them.
  5. Export at 1080p, 30 fps.

CAPCUT MOBILE
  The mobile app can't import .srt files. Either use 0_finished_video_reference.mp4
  (subtitles already burned in), or use Text > Auto captions on the voice-over track
  and correct any wrong words.

TIPS
  * Everything is timed from 0:00, so if the tracks all start together they stay in sync.
  * To replace the computer voice with your own: mute 2_voiceover.mp3 and record over it,
    following the same timing (the .srt shows when each line is spoken).
